# Start here — in plain words

**Not a programmer? This page is for you.**

## What is this thing?

Alexa+ is a brilliant talker. Ask for a song in a mixed-up, accented,
half-remembered way and she understands you. The trouble starts one step
later, at the music catalog: the catalog answers with remixes, karaoke
versions, and songs that share a name with the one you wanted — and
nothing checks the answer before it plays.

**Intent Lock is that check.** It is a free add-on that sits between
Alexa+ and the catalog — *Borrow the Brain, Own the Guarantee.* She does
the understanding (that part is hers, and she is good at it). Intent Lock
proves the exact song, cable, or grocery item against your actual words
before anything plays or gets ordered. Three promises:

1. **It never guesses.** If the evidence is thin it says so. If two items
   genuinely tie, it shows you both by name and lets the choice be made —
   it never just picks one and hopes.
2. **It shows its work.** Every answer comes with a proof card: what it
   heard, what it matched, and every wrong version it rejected — with the
   reason, in plain words.
3. **It costs nothing.** No accounts, no keys, no settings, no bills for
   the checking itself. Ever.

That's the whole idea. Everything else in this folder is the proof,
written for technical judges.

## "I want to install it"

Honest warning: today, installing it yourself needs a *terminal* (the app
where you type commands) and two free programs called Node and Python.
If those words are new to you, this part is honestly not written for you
yet — show this page to any developer friend. Afterwards there is
nothing to configure: you just talk to it.

If you *are* comfortable in a terminal, everything you need is in the
"Quickstart" section of the main README — two commands, nothing else.

## Words in this project that scared you, translated

| The project says | It just means |
|---|---|
| MCP | The standard language the add-on and Alexa+ use to talk to each other |
| Intent Lock | The checker itself — it locks the exact item, or says it can't |
| Trust Card | The proof card that shows what was heard, matched, and rejected |
| Taste Lock | A rule you say once ("after 10 PM, that song means the lofi version") that it remembers |
| tie | Two items scored exactly even — both are shown by name, never guessed between |
| no lock | An honest "I can't prove it" — nothing plays rather than the wrong thing playing |
| −32042 | "Your music account logged itself out" — you get a tap-to-reconnect card |
